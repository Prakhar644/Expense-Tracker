Detailed Metrics

This dashboard aggregates raw sales and expense records into digestible metrics:

  • Total Sales: Sum of all sale totals for the selected period.
  • Total Expenses: Sum of all recorded expenses (salaries, deliveries, utilities, other).
  • Net Profit: Sales minus expenses. Negative values indicate a loss.
  • Monthly Trend: Compare the same month across years to detect seasonality.

How to interpret these numbers

  1. Look at the monthly profit bar chart to identify growth or declines month-over-month.
  2. If expenses are rising faster than sales, inspect categories (salaries, deliveries, utilities) to control spend.
  3. Use the pie chart on the left to understand where money is going in the selected month.
  4. Apply rolling averages (3-month or 12-month) for stable trend analysis — we can add that if you'd like.

Export & Backup

You can export the displayed report as CSV for offline analysis and backup. It's a good practice to keep regular copies of your database file or use the included script at scripts/dump_db.py.

  • CSV export will include month, sales, expenses and net profit.
  • Back up your DB weekly or before major changes.
