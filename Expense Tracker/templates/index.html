About Expense Tracking

Expense Tracking is a modern web application designed specifically for dealers and business owners to simplify and streamline financial management. It provides a seamless, intuitive way to record sales and expenses, categorize transactions with precision, and generate insightful reports that drive decision-making.

Why Choose Expense Tracking?
  • Real-time Financial Visibility — Track your income and expenses as they happen, giving you instant insights into your business health.
  • Easy-to-use Interface — Designed for quick data entry and intuitive review, so you can focus on running your business.
  • Responsive Design — Works seamlessly on desktops, tablets, and mobile devices for on-the-go tracking.
  • Interactive Charts — Visualize your business performance with professional charts and graphs.
Key Features
  • Detailed Breakdowns — Categorized expense and sales analysis for deeper insights into your operations.
  • Monthly & Yearly Trends — Identify patterns to help you plan and grow your business strategically.
  • Secure & Private — Your data stays with you; no cloud dependencies or third-party access.
  • Budget Planning — Set budgets for different expense categories and monitor your actual spending.

Whether you are managing a small showroom or a large dealership, Expense Tracking helps you stay organized, identify cost-saving opportunities, and make smarter financial decisions based on real data. Scroll down to explore features, tips, and your financial summary.

Tips & Best Practices

  • Record sales daily to avoid backlog and errors.
  • Categorize each expense — it makes monthly budgeting accurate.
  • Compare the same month across years to see real growth.
  • Use reports to spot seasonal trends and adjust stock.
  • Track driver costs separately to evaluate delivery efficiency.
  • Back up the database regularly (scripts/dump_db.py can help).
